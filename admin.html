Rules that keep the money low

  • Free first, boost second. Only boost a Facebook post ($5 a day for 5 days, Toms River + 15 miles) if a week of free posting brought fewer than 5 leads.
  • Pay for ZipRecruiter only after the free trial proves it. Under 5 real leads during the trial, cancel before it ends.
  • Referrals are the cheapest hires you will make. Ask current drivers every Thursday to send the apply link to someone reliable.
  • Answer within the hour. Keep WhatsApp Business and Indeed notifications on and use the quick replies from the Posts tab.
  • Say "free training provided" in every post. Most good drivers do not have a CDL yet.
