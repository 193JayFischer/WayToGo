Consistency beats budget

Rules that keep the money low

1
Free first, boost second.

Organic Indeed, Facebook groups, WhatsApp, Instagram, referrals and flyers are all free. Only boost a Facebook post ($5 a day, 5 days, Toms River + 15 miles) if a week of free posting brought fewer than 5 leads.

2
Pay for ZipRecruiter only after the free trial proves it.

Count the leads it sends during the trial in the Leads tab (source = ZipRecruiter). Under 5 real leads, cancel before the trial ends.

3
Referral bonus is the cheapest hire you will make.

Pay current drivers for every hire who lasts 90 days. It costs nothing until it works.

4
Answer within the hour.

Keep WhatsApp Business and Indeed notifications on. Use the quick replies from the Posts tab so a reply takes 10 seconds.

5
Recruit people without a CDL.

Most people who would be good drivers do not have a CDL yet. "Training available" is the one line that makes a post stand out. Say it in every post.
