Job responsibilities

  • Safely operate a school bus on assigned routes and schedules
  • Transport students to and from school and related activities
  • Conduct required pre-trip and post-trip vehicle inspections
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and professional bus environment
  • Monitor student behavior and promote a safe riding experience
  • Communicate effectively with parents, school personnel, and dispatch staff
  • Report maintenance concerns, incidents, accidents, or safety issues promptly
  • Follow all company policies, DOT regulations, and school transportation requirements

Minimum qualifications

  • Valid CDL with Passenger (P) and School Bus (S) endorsements preferred, not required
  • Must be at least 21 years of age
  • Clean driving record
  • Ability to pass a DOT physical examination, drug screening, fingerprinting, and criminal background check
  • Reliable transportation and strong attendance record

Preferred qualifications

  • Previous school bus driving experience
  • Experience transporting students or passengers
  • Strong customer service and communication skills
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure
